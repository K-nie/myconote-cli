    MycoNote competitiveness roadmap¶

    Synthesis of a three-agent research effort (tool-engineering, ML/DL gene finders, community/forum mining) + the 14-genome 4-tool benchmark + verification of what each competitor arm actually ran. Internal planning doc: every external claim carries its source; mechanism-justified gains with no isolated fungal point estimate are marked "measure on our 14-genome set before any manuscript claim."

    1. Diagnosis (verified, not inferred)¶

    Benchmark headline (per-genome collapse, n=5): gene F1 loose — BRAKER3 0.930 > funannotate 0.914 > MAKER 0.906 > MycoNote 0.899; strict — BRAKER 0.659 > funannotate 0.636 > MAKER 0.625 > MycoNote 0.609.

    Two facts reframe the whole problem:

    • The gap is small and it's a strict-F1 (CDS-structure) problem, not loose. Loose: −1.5 pts vs funannotate, −3.1 vs BRAKER. Strict: −5.0 vs BRAKER. The priority is CDS-structure correctness / precision, not gene detection.
    • No tool used RNA-seq (verified in the wrappers). BRAKER3 ran protein-only (OrthoDB Fungi, --prot_seq); funannotate ran BUSCO-seed per-genome Augustus training; MAKER ran pure ab-initio (keep_preds=1); MycoNote ran pure ab-initio consensus (Augustus 3.3.3 + SNAP + GeneMark-ES, agreement-weighted). So BRAKER3's famous ~+20 transcript-F1 RNA-seq lift is not in play here — the gap MycoNote must close is the 1.5–5 pt structural gap, not 20 pts.

    Where each competitor's edge comes from, given what they ran: - vs BRAKER3 → protein evidence (OrthoDB) + TSEBRA transcript selection. - vs funannotate → per-genome Augustus training (BUSCO-seeded) + EVM weighted consensus (not flat agreement). - vs MAKER (also pure ab-initio, yet 0.906 > 0.899) → MycoNote's 3-way consensus isn't currently buying accuracy over one well-configured Augustus.

    The fungal ceiling caveat (most important for triage): in compact, intron-poor fungal genomes the ab-initio ceiling is already high, so evidence-integration pays far less than in vertebrates. GeneMark-EP+ lifted sensitivity on N. crassa (a fungus) by only ~2% even with the largest protein set, "because accuracy of GeneMark-ES was high as typical for fungal genomes" (GeneMark-EP+, NARGAB 2020). This is why MycoNote's --protein-hints A/B found no F1 lift (memory: accuracy_levers_closed) — it is consistent with the literature, largely the data, not just an integration bug.

    2. Prioritized roadmap (ranked by accuracy-gain × feasibility × portability)¶

    Portability-safe, Rust/config-only changes are at the top by design — they preserve MycoNote's genuine edges (fastest prediction, best scaling, runs on any CPU without AVX2).

    #1 — Post-prediction filtering → raise strict F1 (do first; Rust-native, zero portability cost)¶

    Add a filter pass after the consensus: drop single-exon genes predicted purely ab-initio with no cross-predictor agreement; require complete ORFs (start+stop, no internal stop) unless flagged partial; drop single-predictor models below a length/score floor. Expose as --min-support / --keep-single-exon (deterministic). - Precedent: BRAKER3 explicitly removes single-exon purely-ab-initio genes to lift precision (BRAKER3, Genome Res 2024); MAKER's entire QC model is AED-based filtering (AED ≤0.5 "good", a good genome has >90% genes ≤0.5) (MAKER2, BMC Bioinformatics 2011). - Our AED analogue = the cross-predictor agreement we already compute. - Risk: filtering trades sensitivity for precision — tune on the 14 genomes and report F1, not precision alone.

    #2 — Upgrade the combiner: weighted vote + Augustus-HiQ tier (the mechanism funannotate beats us with)¶

    Generalize the combiner from "count agreement" to a weighted vote with a tunable per-predictor weight and a bonus tier for Augustus calls that agree with another predictor (our "high-quality Augustus" analogue). - funannotate runs EVidenceModeler with default weights Augustus=1, Augustus HiQ=2, GeneMark=1, snap=1 — it trusts (high-quality) Augustus more than the rest, and beats us on the same ab-initio inputs (funannotate predict docs; EVidenceModeler, Genome Biol 2008). - Do it Rust-native (keeps the pure-Rust/run-anywhere edge). Wrapping EVM v2 would match funannotate exactly but adds a Perl/bioconda dependency — only for a quick parity check. - Do NOT port TSEBRA for the ab-initio case — it scores transcripts by RNA-seq/protein support (ε/weight thresholds over evidence); with no evidence it degenerates (TSEBRA, BMC Bioinformatics 2021). TSEBRA is the right model for our RNA-seq mode, not this benchmark. - Gain is mechanism-justified (no isolated fungal point estimate) → measure first.

    #3 — Pfam annotate speed: confirm threading/chunking (we already use hmmsearch)¶

    MycoNote already prefers hmmsearch over hmmscan (src/annotate/pfam.rs), so the big hmmscan→hmmsearch I/O win (Eddy, cryptogenomicon) is already banked. The remaining lever is re-parallelization: hmmsearch leaves ~65% of work serial, and wrappers that re-chunk it (pyhmmer) cut a 97 h run to 27 h on 6 cores (PyHMMER, Bioinformatics 2023). Shard proteins across cores + tune --cpu/-Z. Zero portability cost. - Optional, flag-gated only: nail (Rust pHMM, ~26× HMMER, not SIMD-vectorized) (nail, PMC10862755) — but it requires MMseqs2 (a C++ binary), which erodes the pure-Rust/any-CPU edge. Keep hmmsearch the default; expose --pfam-backend {hmmsearch,nail} only if #3's free parallelization isn't enough.

    #4 — RNA-seq benchmark arm + repositioning (where the real accuracy lives)¶

    MycoNote already ships RNA-seq (v0.7.3); the benchmark just didn't use it. Add a second benchmark arm with RNA-seq so the paper isn't comparing MycoNote-ab-initio against tools in their evidence sweet spot, and reposition docs to "if you have reads, use them." The ~20-pt lift is an RNA-seq effect (BRAKER3). Harness work → bench-harness-engineer. No portability change.

    #5 — Tiberius as an optional benchmark-gated predictor (the one DL bet)¶

    For fungi, DL is not the obvious win it is for plants/vertebrates: Helixer only ties Augustus/GeneMark-ES on fungi (benchmark-only). Tiberius is the one worth testing — fungal ab-initio within ~3 pts of BRAKER3, dedicated fungal model (Tiberius multi-clade, PMC13142403). Integration shape has a precedent: funannotate2 runs Helixer standalone → takes its GFF3 → feeds EVM as high-weight evidence; same adapter pattern fits our combiner. - Portability cost: pulls in a TensorFlow stack (CPU wheels need AVX); no hard GPU requirement at fungal sizes but it dents the single-binary/any-CPU story → ship as --predictor tiberius, never a default. - Data-leakage guard: Tiberius/Helixer ship pretrained fungal models whose training species may overlap our panel — hold those genomes out when benchmarking.

    #6 — Document the known-nulls / hard limits (honesty, cheap)¶

    • Protein hints give ~2% in fungi (ceiling above) → document as known-null; only revisit with evolutionarily-close proteins + confidence-tiered hints (miniprot + miniprothint, the GALBA recipe) and still expect low single digits (GALBA 2023; miniprot).
    • tmel (125 Mb) is a hard limit for all tools (~0.52–0.61 F1): >58% TEs + RIP (fungal C→T genome defense that destroys homology signal) (T. melanosporum genome; RIP). Frame it as a known-hard outlier — MycoNote collapses like the others, not worse.
    • Repeat-rich genomes need a de-novo RepeatModeler library + soft-mask (never hard-mask — it buries exons) (Galaxy masking). An adaptive filter (lenient for compact genomes, aggressive for Tuber-like) is funannotate's documented design (--repeat_filter off by default, on for high-repeat genomes).

    3. Portability guardrails (the edges we must NOT trade away)¶

    • Keep Augustus 3.3.3 the default (runs on any CPU). Competitors need AVX2-compiled 3.5.0. At most offer 3.5.0 as an opt-in fast path for AVX2 hosts.
    • Keep hmmsearch the portable Pfam default; nail/MMseqs2 behind a flag only.
    • Keep the combiner Rust-native (avoid EVM's Perl / Tiberius's TF as hard deps).

    • 1 (filtering), #2-route-1 (weighted combiner), and #3 (hmmsearch parallelism)¶

      are all portability-neutral — which is exactly why they lead.

    4. The honest positioning (for the paper)¶

    Not "faster" or "more accurate." The defensible story: competitive accuracy (within 1.5–5 F1 pts on ab-initio inputs), fastest prediction stage, best scaling to large genomes, runs on any CPU, complete one-tool pipeline to NCBI submission. The roadmap closes the strict-F1 gap with portability-safe changes first.

    5. Immediate next actions (suggested order)¶

    1. 1 filtering + #3 hmmsearch parallelism — both free, portability-safe → implement, test on the 14 genomes.¶

    2. 2 Rust-native weighted combiner (Augustus-HiQ tier) — the funannotate mechanism, kept pure-Rust.¶

    3. 4 RNA-seq benchmark arm (bench-harness-engineer) — the honest accuracy story.¶

    4. 5 Tiberius optional, benchmark-gated with leakage holdout.¶

    5. 6 document the fungal protein-hint ceiling + the tmel hard limit.¶
